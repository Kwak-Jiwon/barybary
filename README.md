# 바리바리


## 👨‍🏫 Project Introduction
"barybary" is an app designed to help coffee enthusiasts delve deeper into the world of coffee and enhance their sensory experience. This all-in-one platform allows users to enjoy, record, and master the art of brewing the perfect cup of drip coffee. Our goal is to maximize the sensory journey of coffee lovers and provide a richer, more emotional coffee experience. ☕✨

- **Server** : AWS EC2
- **DataBase** : MongoDB
- **back-end** : Django
- **front-end** : flutter


## 📌 Key Features
- Cupping Note Page
  - This page allows users to write detailed reviews about the coffee they’ve tasted. Users can record the coffee’s aroma, flavor, and body in an emotional and personalized way, crafting their unique coffee profile.
  - Select Various Flavor Notes: Choose from floral, fruity, berry, nutty, cereal, chocolate, and more.
  - Evaluate Attributes: Use sliders to rate sweetness, acidity, bitterness, and body.
  - Rate and Record: Assign an overall score and save it for future reference.

- Rating Page
   - A page where you can rate the coffee you’ve tasted and share your experiences with others. Record your impressions of various coffees and discover your unique coffee preferences.
   - Star Ratings: Rate each coffee with a star-based system.
   - Personal Coffee Rankings: View a list of coffees you’ve rated and create your own coffee ranking.

- Recipe Page
    - Follow barista-recommended brewing recipes and enjoy a perfect drip coffee experience with a timer and soothing background music. Make your coffee-brewing moments truly special.
    - Explore Recipes: Discover various drip coffee recipes recommended by professional baristas.
    - Interactive Timer: Select a recipe to activate a timer synchronized with the brewing process, accompanied by relaxing music.
    - Tools and Ingredients: Access a detailed list of tools and ingredients required for each recipe.

- My Reviews
    - A dedicated page that compiles all your coffee reviews, allowing you to reflect on your coffee journey over time.
    - View at a Glance: See all your cupping notes and ratings in one place.
    - Sort and Filter: Organize your reviews by various criteria and apply filters to find specific entries.
